RIDGE: Role-Adaptive Routing for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems

提出轻量级角色自适应模型路由框架：利用在线 Critic 评估和故障归因构建六维执行风险状态，再由角色条件超网络预测不同模型的执行风险；在线 MemoryBank 辅助决策。面向 SWE-bench Lite 与 WebArena-Verified 评估。

A lightweight role-adaptive router that derives six-dimensional execution-risk states from online Critic evaluations and forensic attribution. A role-conditioned hypernetwork estimates model-specific risks, supported by an online MemoryBank. Evaluated on SWE-bench Lite and WebArena-Verified.

核心结果：强模型 Token 使用量最多降低 41.6%；76.19% 的失败关键步骤被路由至强模型（OmniRouter：21.99%；RouteLLM：15.06%）。Key results: up to 41.6% fewer strong-model tokens; 76.19% of failure-critical steps routed to strong models (OmniRouter: 21.99%; RouteLLM: 15.06%).
2026ARXIV
预印本PreprintCost-Aware Routing

CASTER: Breaking the Cost-Performance Barrier in Multi-Agent Orchestration via Context-Aware Strategy for Task Efficient Routing

提出面向图结构多智能体系统的轻量级动态路由方法，通过语义嵌入与结构元特征组成双信号路由器，并采用从冷启动到迭代进化的优化过程，利用路由失败反馈更新决策。

A context-aware dual-signal router for graph-based MAS, combining semantic embeddings with structural meta-features. A cold-start-to-iterative-evolution training strategy learns from routing failures using on-policy negative feedback.

核心结果：在软件工程、数据分析、科学发现和网络安全等任务上，与强模型基线相比，推理成本最多降低 72.4%。Key result: up to 72.4% lower inference cost than strong-model baselines across software engineering, data analysis, scientific discovery, and cybersecurity.
